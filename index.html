Split a big PDF into parts that fit.

Every slide is copied exactly as it is. Nothing is re-compressed, resized or recoloured. Pages stay in order, and each part stays under the size you choose. Everything runs in your browser.

MB

Parts are kept slightly under this, so they fit limits on WhatsApp, email and upload sites.

Why splitting keeps quality perfect

Each page is moved into a new file with its images, fonts and graphics byte-for-byte identical. If several slides share an image or font, each part gets its own copy, so every part opens on its own.

A single slide bigger than your limit can't be split further without changing it. It gets a part of its own, marked in orange.
